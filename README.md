# AnimeSavi

AnimeStreamHub is an anime streaming app designed for anime fans where only designated uploaders can upload content. Viewers can watch their favorite anime and anime movies, with features like language switching, season selection, and a unique in-app video player experience. Uploaders can share their own anime or anime movies, customize titles and descriptions, and even share anime from external sources when they don't have their own uploads.

Key Features:

User Roles:

Uploader: The only role that can upload anime content. Uploaders can share their own anime or anime movies, add custom titles, descriptions, and even provide external anime links from trusted sources.

Viewer: Regular users who can only watch content, browse anime collections, switch languages, and select seasons of ongoing anime. They cannot upload any content.

In-App Purchases:

Premium Subscription: Unlock premium features such as access to high-definition streaming, early access to newly uploaded anime, and exclusive anime titles.

Season Pass: Users can purchase season passes to automatically unlock all episodes for a specific anime season.

Custom Themes: Personalize the app’s look with paid themes and skins.

Ad-Free Experience: Purchase an ad-free subscription for uninterrupted watching.

Upload Anime Content:

Upload Own Anime or Movie: Uploaders can submit their personal anime series or movies with a unique title, detailed description, and an uploaded video.

External Anime Links: If the uploader doesn't have a personal video, they can insert a link to external sources (trusted anime websites) to share the content.

Title and Description Customization: Each uploaded anime will have a custom title, description, and cover image, making it stand out.

Language Switcher:
Viewers can easily change the language (subtitles or dub) depending on their preference. The app supports multiple languages such as English, Japanese, Spanish, French, and more.

Season Change Option:

Viewers can choose specific seasons and episodes of ongoing anime. This feature is helpful for anime that has multiple seasons or is ongoing (e.g., One Piece, Naruto).

A season drop-down menu will be available for easy navigation to switch between seasons.

Custom Video Player:

Adaptive Streaming: The video player adjusts streaming quality based on internet speed to ensure smooth playback.

Subtitles: Customizable subtitle size and font, as well as color options to suit viewer preferences.

Picture-in-Picture Mode: Watch anime while browsing other parts of the app.

Playback Speed: Users can adjust the playback speed of the video (e.g., 1.5x, 2x).

Bookmarking: Save your place in an episode so you can pick up right where you left off.

Content Discovery:

Trending and Recommended: A dynamic feed of trending and recommended anime based on user preferences and previous watching history.

Genre Filters: Browse anime by genre like Action, Romance, Horror, Comedy, etc.

Search Bar: A powerful search tool to find anime by title, year, or genre.

Notifications & Updates:

Get notified when a new episode is uploaded or when a season is updated.

Uploaders will receive notifications about their uploaded content, including views, likes, and comments.

User Journey:

Viewer:

Open the app, browse anime, and filter by genre, season, or trending.

Switch language options for subtitles/dubs.

Watch anime in the custom player with options to change video quality, subtitle language, or playback speed.

Purchase premium features like ad-free experience, season passes, or high-definition quality.

Uploader:

Open the app and log in with a verified uploader account.

Upload anime or anime movies, fill in the title, description, and cover image.

Choose to upload directly or paste a link to external anime sources.

Manage uploaded content, see views, likes, and receive notifications for interactions.

Additional Features:

Profile Pages: Both uploaders and viewers have a profile page showing their activity (watched shows, favorite genres, liked content).

In-App Chat/Comments: Viewers can comment on episodes, and uploaders can respond to feedback.

Dark Mode: Switch to a dark theme for easier viewing at night.

Offline Watching: Premium users can download episodes to watch offline.

App Design:

Minimalist and User-Friendly Interface: The interface will be sleek, intuitive, and easy to navigate, with a vibrant, anime-inspired theme.

Mobile & Tablet Optimized: The app is fully optimized for mobile devices and tablets, ensuring smooth, high-quality video playback across devices.
